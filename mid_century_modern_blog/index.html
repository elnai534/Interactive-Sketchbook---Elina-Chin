mid-century modern

02/14/2024

a visual guide to mid-century modern style

I have decided to put my reflection here! This assignment was very fun to play around with, it involed many elements and I feel like it really helped me apply all the previous assignments to this one. The hardest part for me was figuring out the footer and the spacing, and I could not get it exactly. I tried opening this up in inspector and it really helped with the padding + margins. I feel like the part I learned about the most was CSS. I feel like applying it with a very certain goal in mind really made me try different things. I had to google the CSS docs and use those to help me there were a lot of things that I did not know the syntax for. The part that took me the longest was definitely the footer, I had to figure out how to make it stay at the bottom of the page and not move up when I added more content. I also had to figure out how to make the images smaller and not take up the whole page.
